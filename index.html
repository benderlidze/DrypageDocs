Drypage

A sober calendar you tear by hand.

Drypage is an alcohol tracker built on a real tear-off calendar, for iPhone and Android. Every day there is one page. Tap in how many drinks you had, tear the page off, and it joins your month in the colour of the day: dry days in green, heavier days in warmer colours.

It is not a countdown that resets the first time you slip. It is an honest record of every day, with your dry streak, your best streak and the share of alcohol-free days, for Dry January, moderation, or quitting.

There is no account and no ads. Your log stays on your device.
