Privacy Policy

Drypage for iPhone and Android · last updated 7 October 2026

Drypage does not ask you to create an account and shows no ads. Your drinking log lives on your device. A small amount of information does leave it: anonymous usage analytics, the details of a purchase, and anything you choose to send through the feedback form. Each is described below, along with why.

What stays on your device

  • The pages you have torn off, the dates they belong to and the number of drinks on each.
  • The notes you write on the back of a page.
  • Your answers from the first-launch questions, your reminder times and your display settings.

All of it is kept in the app's own storage on the device (and in the home screen widget, which reads the same storage). It is not uploaded to us, and deleting the app deletes it. The text of your notes is never sent anywhere.

Usage analytics

To understand which parts of the app are used and where people get stuck, Drypage sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, Inc., which processes them on our behalf. Events are tied to a random identifier created for your installation, not to your name, email or phone number. They describe what happened in the app, for example that a page was torn, that a screen or the paywall was opened, or that a purchase completed. One event carries the length of a note (never its text). Events do not include how many drinks you log, the goal or weekly starting point you choose in the first-launch questions, or anything you write.

Analytics are not used for advertising and are not combined with data from other apps or websites. PostHog's privacy policy is at posthog.com/privacy.

Reminders

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled by your device. The reminder time is not sent to a server.

If you send feedback

When you use the feedback form, your message is sent to our server together with the app version and your device model, so a bug report can be made sense of. An email address is optional and is only used to reply to you. Feedback is not used for advertising and is not shared.

If you subscribe or buy Premium

Payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card, and we are not told your name or your Apple or Google account.

Purchases are managed for us by RevenueCat, Inc., which receives the anonymous identifier of your installation and the details of the purchase itself (which product, when, whether it is still active) so the app can tell whether Premium is yours. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties. PostHog and RevenueCat process data only on our behalf.
  • No medical advice. Drypage is a self-tracking tool, not a medical device.

Children

Drypage is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Your choices and deleting your data

Reminders can be turned off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time. "Reset everything" in the app's settings clears your pages, drink counts, notes and the app's local state; deleting the app removes all of it from the device.

To have the analytics events linked to your installation or the feedback you sent us deleted, email benderlio@gmail.com with the subject "Drypage data deletion". Tell us roughly when you installed the app and, if you sent feedback, the email you used. We delete the matching records within 30 days. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google and RevenueCat as required for accounting.
