Drypage Support

A sober calendar you tear by hand.

Write to us

Anything at all, a bug or an idea: benderlio@gmail.com. You can also use Settings → Send feedback inside the app, which sends the same message along with your app version so a problem is easier to find. We answer in English.

Common questions

How do I log a day?

Tap − or + on today's page to set how many drinks you had, then tear the page off by drawing a finger across it. It joins your month in the colour of the day.

I missed a few days. What now?

The calendar waits on the first page you have not logged. Tear each one off with its count and you are caught up.

How do I fix a day I got wrong?

Open it from History and change the count with the same counter.

How do I change or turn off reminders?

In Settings. Each reminder has its own switch and time.

I paid, and the app still asks me to.

Open the paywall and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to the Apple or Google account you bought them with, so make sure you are signed in with that one.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Drypage. Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Drypage. Cancelling stops the renewal; the time you have already paid for stays yours.

I lost my pages after reinstalling.

Your log lives on the device only, so deleting the app deletes it. Your purchase is not lost: restore it as above.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Reset everything clears everything on the device. To delete analytics or feedback we hold, see the privacy policy.
